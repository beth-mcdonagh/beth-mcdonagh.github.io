## Short CV
I've been a postdoctoral researcher in the ROFS (Regional Ocean Forecasting Systems) division of [CMCC](https://www.cmcc.it/ "CMCC") since 2024, after finishing my PhD in the programme “Future Earth, Climate Change and Societal Challenge” at the University of Bologna with supervision from CMCC. I completed my MSc in Atmosphere, Ocean and Climate at the University of Reading in 2020.

During my PhD, I studied the effects of barotropic and internal tides on the Mediterranean Sea circulation through numerical experiments. 

Currently, I work between two projects: one leading the research and development of an ocean ensemble forecasting system in the Mediterranean Sea, and the other using a nested modelling approach to integrate kilometre-scale horizontal resolution in the Gibraltar Strait within a forecasting model of the Mediterranean Sea. 


## Research interests
My research interests include understanding predictability and variability of the ocean, tides and internal waves, their interactions with other phenomena, high-resolution modelling of the ocean, the general circulation of the Mediterranean Sea, and the Gibraltar Strait dynamics.
